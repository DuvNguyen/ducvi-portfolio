# Project Rules: Portfolio Voice, Tone & UI/UX Design

## 1. No Instructional / Patronizing Meta-Copy
- The user interface and visual hierarchy must speak for itself through intuitive UI/UX design.
- Never write instructive, patronizing, or "AI-style" meta-text explaining to visitors what they should do or what a button/card is for (e.g., avoid "60-second snapshot for Hiring Managers", "Recruiter Fast-Track", "Need a 1-page resume or a quick chat?", "Are you hiring? Click here", "Actively interviewing — response guaranteed within 24 hours").
- Action elements, navigation, and buttons must use clean, confident, objective labels (e.g. `Projects`, `Blog`, `About`, `CV`, `Email`, `LinkedIn`).

## 2. Design System & Visual Consistency (Soft Bento Minimalism)
- Stick strictly to the authentic Soft Bento aesthetic: neutral warm grays (`bg-canvas`), card surfaces (`#FFFFFF`), charcoal text (`#121214`), and soft borders (`border-black/[0.06]`, `border-zinc-200/80`).
- Avoid loud, artificial green badges (`bg-emerald-50`, `border-emerald-200`, `text-emerald-800`) or pulsating dots that look like generic AI chatbot badges. Visuals must be harmonious, calm, and professional.
